## Structure

- lib.rs declares the modules and re-exports the public API; main.rs is the binary entry point that parses subcommands and runs the TUI event loop through the Session struct.
- descent.rs drives one pact, refresh or unpact walk with a single manifest save, and forwards engine callbacks as RunEvent through the Relay observer.
- running.rs runs headless pact and refresh through started, composing descended, Progress, Report and ending; pacting.rs drives the same runs in the TUI on a worker thread and drains PactEvent into panel sections.
- boundary.rs holds the shared verdict logic for sigil-scope refusals, used by both the panel and headless doors; session.rs closed_scope enforces it on p, r and s, and edits.rs checks it through Opened.
- session.rs load_app and reload build and refresh the App tree, manifest and Chrome; standing.rs supplies the working directory, repository root and Manifest they load.
- input.rs routes keys and mouse into Pressed via press_for; the per-key handlers are editing.rs, viewing.rs, scoping.rs, pacting.rs and pushing.rs, with prompt.rs and confirm.rs holding modal state.
- ui.rs draw paints the App, panel, composer and dialogs; colour.rs supplies the colours, and wrap.rs and selection.rs shape and map the panel text.
- chatting.rs runs the panel conversation: submission.rs classifies a draft into Submitted, and agent turns run in the background via claude.rs, recorded in thread.rs.
- writing.rs turns a model's brief reply into a numbered file, checked against template.rs missing_sections; brief.rs loads and validates a brief.
- push.rs files a brief to Linear headlessly and pushing.rs does it from the TUI, both through the linear.rs Client.
- stubs.rs and fixture.rs are test doubles and hand-written Tree fixtures for app, draw and chat tests.
- check.rs, key.rs, config.rs, query.rs and edits.rs implement the check, key, config, stale/fresh and unpact/scope subcommands; error.rs defines the crate-wide Error.
