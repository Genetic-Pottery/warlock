## Structure

- lib.rs: crate root re-exporting the front end's modules (account, app, claude, colour, composer, confirm, panel, prompt, selection, submission, template, thread, ui, watch) — no terminal handling of its own.
- main.rs: Cli/Command parsing, main()'s dispatch, Session<S,P,C,B> struct, run() event loop, apply_mouse, keep_up
- App: front-end state — flattens a Tree into rows and derives the drawn view by filters
- session.rs: load_app/reload build and refresh the App's tree, manifest and Chrome, and closed_scope enforces the boundary on p/r/s.
- Shared boundary logic for panel and headless doors: Reach, Verdict enums and verdict() decide sigil-scope refusals.
- descend() drive one pact/refresh/unpact walk; RunEvent and the Relay observer forward engine callbacks to the panel/shell sink.
- pacting.rs drives the p and r keys via drain() into panel sections and the single post-run reload.
- chatting.rs: Chat<C> drives /brief, /chat, /write turns via submission.rs's Submitted classification and thread.rs's Thread.
- claude.rs spawns claude as a child process, streaming into an agent::Response consumed by chatting.rs.
- ui.rs draws one frame purely from arguments, laying out panel.rs, composer.rs, confirm.rs and prompt.rs state plus wrap.rs text wrapping.
- input.rs turns key and mouse events into Action/Pressed/MouseAction, consumed by editing.rs, scoping.rs and viewing.rs's key handlers.
- check.rs, query.rs and running.rs implement warlock check/stale/fresh/pact/refresh via boundary.rs and descent.rs, sharing standing.rs's repo_root resolution.
