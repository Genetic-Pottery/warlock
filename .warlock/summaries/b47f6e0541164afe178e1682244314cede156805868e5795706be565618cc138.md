Structure and content, in order:

1. **Module doc comment** explaining the panel's purpose: it holds three "cards" (Account, Thread/conversation, Document), of which exactly one is shown at a time, and that this module was extracted from a monolithic `App` struct to make panel behavior testable independently of the rest of the app (tree, selection, scroll state). It explains why `App` still forwards methods to this module.

2. **`Panel` struct**: holds `account: Card<Account>`, `thread: Card<Thread>`, `document: Card<Vec<Line>>`, `showing: Showing`, `mode: Mode`, `height: usize`, `width: usize`. Extensive doc comments explain: why this group survives a "reload"/rollback, why each card persists once filled, why a run is written only to its own card, the distinction between "filled" and "having lines," when `showing` changes (only three triggers: view key, message submit, swap key), and how `offset`/`follows` per-card windowing works (mirroring the tree's viewport/scroll-offset but with a follow flag instead of a selection).

3. **`Showing` enum**: `Account`, `Thread` (default), `Document` — three closed variants, no "nothing" case (empty card just draws a mark). Has a `next()` method defining swap-key order (Thread → Account → Document → Thread).

4. **`Mode` enum**: `Chat` (default) and `Brief`, representing conversation register; documented as affecting only which instruction/model/effort is used, not the thread's stored data, and drawn only in the panel's border title.

5. **`Card<T>` struct**: generic holder with `held: Option<T>`, `offset: usize`, `follows: bool`. Manual `Default` impl (since `T` may have none, e.g. `Account`). Methods: `place` (replace contents, reset window), `accrue` (get-or-insert, always sets follow), `line_count`, `scroll_offset`, `window`, `lines_below`, `scroll_to`.

6. **`Shown` trait**: `line_count(width)` and `window(offset, height, width, now)`, implemented for `Account`, `Thread`, and `Vec<Line>` — each explaining how wrapping/rows-per-width and clock-timing (`now`) interact with row counting.

7. **`rows_of` helper**: delegates to `crate::wrap::rows` to compute wrapped rows for a line, shared by counting and drawing.

8. **`impl Panel` (core)**: `scroll_offset()`, `window(now)`, `lines_below()`, `scroll_to(offset)` — all dispatch on `self.showing` to the matching card.

9. **`panel_offset_for` free function**: pure function computing scroll offset from `(lines, viewport, offset, following)`, documented as distinct from the tree's minimum-movement scrolling rule; handles zero-viewport and following/parked cases.
